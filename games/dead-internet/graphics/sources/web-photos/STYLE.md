# Common style prompt (WEB photos, image generator instead of ComfyUI)

STYLE = "Realistic documentary photograph taken in Port Calder, a fictional small North-Atlantic harbour city (red-brick terraces, cobbles, grey water, harbour cranes in the distance). Overcast pewter-grey sky, soft flat diffuse daylight, it has just rained: wet tarmac and pavements with soft reflections. Muted cool palette of slate blue, grey and weathered red brick with small warm accents (lit windows, amber lamps). Natural colours, slight sensor noise, gentle contrast, no HDR, not oversharpened, no cartoon, no 3d-render look. Eye-level framing, as a resident or a small local business would shoot it. Absolutely no readable text, no lettering on signs, no logos, no real brands, no watermark. Match the colour grade and light of the reference photos exactly."

Style references always passed: web0026-calder-transit-bus.webp, web0027-harbor-authority.webp (+ web0010 / nimg0003 / nimg0005 for continuity where the brief says so).
Phone-photo variants add: "shot on a mid-range 2019/2021/2022 smartphone, slightly soft, small sensor noise, auto white balance a little cool, casual handheld framing".
Old variants add: "low-resolution 2014 digital compact / scanned faded 1987 colour print".
